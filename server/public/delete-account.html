Delete your account

Use this page to permanently delete your Nexus account and associated data. Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

How to delete your account

1
Enter your Nexus username and password in the form below.
2
Click Delete my account to confirm. No further confirmation is required.
3
Your account will be deleted immediately. You will see a confirmation once complete.
✦
You can also delete your account from within the app: Settings → Profile → Delete my account

Immediately deleted

  • Your account credentials (username, password hash)
  • Your profile information (avatar, bio, status)
  • All session tokens — you are logged out everywhere
  • Your server memberships and role assignments
  • Your friend relationships, blocks, and pending requests
  • Any servers you own that have no other members
  • Your bookmarks and personal notification settings

Retained (anonymised)

  • Messages you sent in servers and DMs — these remain visible but display as "Deleted User" with no link to your account
  • Servers you owned are transferred to the next eligible member
This cannot be undone. Once deleted, your account and all data listed above are permanently removed. There is no grace period or recovery option.
