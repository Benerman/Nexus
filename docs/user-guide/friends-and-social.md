# Friends & Social

## Friends

### Sending a Friend Request

1. Click on a user's profile card
2. Click **Add Friend**
3. The other user receives a friend request notification

You cannot send a friend request to yourself or to someone who has blocked you.

### Managing Friend Requests

Go to the **Friends** tab in your personal area to see:
- **Pending** — Incoming requests to accept or decline
- **All Friends** — Your current friends list
- **Online** — Friends currently online

### Removing Friends

Click on a friend → **Remove Friend** to unfriend them.

## Blocking

Block a user to prevent them from:
- Sending you DMs or friend requests
- Seeing your online status

To block: Click a user's profile → **Block**
To unblock: **Settings → Friends → Blocked** → **Unblock**

## Reporting

Report a user for violations:

1. Click on the user's profile
2. Click **Report**
3. Provide a reason

Reports are stored for platform administrator review.

## Online Status

Your friends can see your online status. Status options:
- **Online** — Active
- **Idle** — Away
- **Do Not Disturb** — No notifications
- **Invisible** — Appear offline
