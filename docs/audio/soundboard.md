# Soundboard

Play sound effects in voice channels for fun interactions.

## Playing Sounds

1. Join a voice channel
2. Open the soundboard panel
3. Click a sound to play it

Sounds are played to all users in the voice channel (or targeted to specific users with the `sendTargetedSounds` permission).

## Built-in Sounds

Nexus includes a set of built-in sound effects available in every server.

## Custom Sounds

Upload custom sounds to your server's soundboard. See [Custom Sounds](custom-sounds.md).

## Rate Limits

Soundboard playback is rate limited to **10 plays per 10 seconds** to prevent spam.

## Permissions

| Permission | Required For |
|------------|-------------|
| — | Playing sounds to the whole channel |
| `sendTargetedSounds` | Playing sounds targeted at specific users |
